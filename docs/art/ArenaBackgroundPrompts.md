# Arena Background Art — Generation Prompts

Ten backgrounds for the replay arenas (Gauntlet floors + Contract sites).

**Specs for every image:**
- Portrait orientation, as tall as your generator allows (target ratio ≈ 1 : 2.2
  — e.g. 1024×2048 or 1024×1792; existing backgrounds are 853×1844). The game
  stretches to fit, so exact size doesn't matter, aspect roughly does.
- Top-down / high-angle view of a single room interior, like looking down into
  a diorama. The playable floor fills the middle ~70% — keep it relatively
  clean and readable (the hex grid, crates, and characters render ON TOP of it).
  Push the dramatic detail to the edges: walls, machinery, signage, railings.
- Dark, moody cyberpunk palette with neon accents. No people, no creatures,
  no text/lettering, no UI elements, no visible grid.
- File naming: save as `arena_01.png` … `arena_10.png` and drop them in
  ~/Downloads — they get imported to `Sprites/backgrounds/` from there.

**Shared style prefix** (paste before each prompt):
> Top-down high-angle view of a cyberpunk interior room, dark moody lighting
> with neon accents, photorealistic digital painting, portrait orientation,
> clean open floor area in the center, dramatic environmental detail around
> the edges, no people, no text.

**arena_01 — Freight Bay**
Corporate freight loading bay at night: stacked shipping crates and cargo
containers along the walls, yellow hazard striping on the concrete floor, a
powered-down loader mech in a corner, cold blue-white industrial floodlights,
a big closed cargo door glowing red at the top of the room.

**arena_02 — Neon Court**
Rain-slick open courtyard between megacity towers: wet asphalt reflecting pink
and cyan neon signage from surrounding walls, steam rising from street vents,
scattered market stalls shuttered for the night along the edges, a narrow
alley exit at the top glowing green.

**arena_03 — Server Vault**
High-security server vault: rows of black server racks with blinking teal
LEDs along both side walls, raised metal floor panels with cable runs, cold
white spot lighting from above, a heavy vault door at the top edge with a
holographic lock display.

**arena_04 — Chem Works**
Industrial chemical plant floor: rusted pipes and valve clusters along the
walls, yellow chemical drums grouped in corners, green-tinged toxic haze in
the air, grated metal flooring with glowing runoff channels, an emergency
exit door at the top bathed in orange warning light.

**arena_05 — Transit Platform**
Abandoned underground metro platform: cracked tile walls covered in glowing
graffiti tags, a dead train visible along one edge, flickering fluorescent
tubes, support pillars casting long shadows, a maintenance door at the top of
the platform lit by a single red bulb.

**arena_06 — Night Market**
Cramped black-market bazaar after hours: canvas awnings and hanging paper
lanterns around the edges, cluttered vendor stalls with tarps, strings of
warm bulbs crossing overhead, wet stone floor, a curtained doorway exit at
the top glowing violet.

**arena_07 — Foundry Floor**
Heavy industry foundry: glowing orange molten metal channels along the side
walls, massive chain hoists and crucibles overhead, soot-black floor plates,
shower of sparks from a far corner, a blast door at the top edge lit by
furnace glow.

**arena_08 — Casino Mezzanine**
Ruined luxury casino mezzanine: overturned card tables and slot machines
pushed to the walls, torn red carpet with gold trim over the open center,
shattered chandelier light, holographic dice adverts flickering on the walls,
a gilded double door at the top under a broken EXIT hologram.

**arena_09 — Rooftop Greenhouse**
Corporate rooftop greenhouse at night: rows of bioluminescent plants in glass
planters along the edges, condensation-fogged glass panel walls with the
megacity skyline glowing beyond, drip irrigation lines overhead, a glass door
at the top edge leading to the roof, moonlight and soft green plant glow.

**arena_10 — Flooded Substation**
Half-flooded electrical substation: shallow dark water covering the floor
with neon reflections, transformer banks and coiled cables along the walls,
sparking junction box in one corner, catwalk grating at the edges, a bulkhead
door at the top edge above the waterline, teal emergency lighting.
